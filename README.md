# 📊Sales & Market Analysics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **Profit & Loss (P&L) performance** using Microsoft Excel.

The main objective is to understand how different markets and sub-zones are performing based on their **Net Sales, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Gross Margin, and Gross Margin Percentage (GM%)**.

The analysis also compares **quarterly Gross Margin %** across different sub-zones and financial years.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

→ Analyze sales performance across different markets

→ Understand the cost involved in generating sales

→ Calculate and analyze Gross Margin

→ Compare Gross Margin % across markets

→ Analyze Gross Margin % by quarter

→ Compare profitability across different sub-zones

→ Identify markets and regions with stronger or weaker profitability

---

## 📂 Workbook Structure

The Excel workbook contains two main analysis sections:

### 1. P & L Year – Markets

This section analyzes financial performance at the **market/country level**.

The main metrics include:

→ Net Sales

→ COGS

→ Gross Margin

→ Gross Margin %

The analysis also includes filters for:

→ Region

→ Sub Zone

→ Financial Year (FY)

---

### 2. GM% by Quarters – Sub Zone

This section analyzes **Gross Margin Percentage by quarter** for different sub-zones.

The quarters analyzed are:

→ Q1

→ Q2

→ Q3

→ Q4

The analysis also provides a **Grand Total GM%** for each sub-zone.

# 📈 Business Value

The analysis can help businesses understand their financial performance and identify areas that may require further investigation.

For example:

A market with **high sales but low GM%** may require an investigation into product costs, pricing, discounts, or other factors affecting profitability.

A market with **lower sales but higher GM%** may have better profitability efficiency.

Therefore, looking at both **Sales and GM% together** provides better insight than looking at sales alone.
